How a round works

Every match is a short burst of sneaking. Hiders try to stay out of sight until the timer hits zero. Catchers sniff out every last pet.

1

Hide

The Catcher counts. Walk into a shelf, box, pet bed, carrier, dryer or pod to vanish inside it, or slip through squeeze gaps that only hiders can use. Once you're hidden, peek to see where the Catchers are.

Hiding spotsSqueeze gapsPeeking
2

Seek

Catchers sniff spots and follow paw prints. If one finds yours, burst out and run. Sprinting is fast but noisy, and a sneeze can be heard across the room. In the final 30 seconds hidden hearts start to pound, so change spots now and then.

Rescue

Caught pets go to the glass Sin Bin. Play a break-out mini-game to escape, or wait for a friend to stand on the glowing rescue pad and free everyone. Sometimes a caught pet gets the offer to turn Turncoat and hunt for the other side.

Meet the pets

Sixteen baby cyborg pets, each with its own name, story and robot parts. Pick your favourite before every match, then dress it up in the Wardrobe.

Every pet that comes through the doors gets the same friendly upgrade from the Robo-Vet: a glowing cyan eye, a light-up collar, one robot leg or arm and a little chest core. The upgrades work a bit too well. At 22:00 the shutters roll down and the pets climb out of their display pods to play hide-and-seek. The rule is simple: be back in your pod before the doors open at 08:00, and never leave a friend behind.

Tap a pet to read its story.

Every pet plays the same, so pick the one you like best.

Ten ways to play

Every mode has its own settings in Custom Match, from tag-back timers to how dark Blackout gets. Quick Play picks a mode and map for you.

Seven places to sneak through

Seven maps are ready for offline play. Online rooms currently use the Mega Store, Robo-Vet, Warehouse and Sky Garden.

21 gadgets and tricks

Glowing boxes hold items. Switch between the ones you're carrying and use them at the right moment. Custom Match sets how many of each family appear.

Special items and machines

Win, find or buy special items, keep them in your Inventory and take one into a match. Each one works once: use it and it's gone. Get caught before you use it and you'll get it back when you're freed. There are 28 to collect: 16 from the pets' toy boxes and 12 store items.

Collect them

Items come in three rarities: Common (7 in 10), Rare (1 in 4) and Legendary (1 in 20). You can hold up to 5 of each and find up to 6 a day. Any extras turn into 10 bolts. Equip one on the Inventory page before a match.

Bolt tokens

Spinning nuts pop up on the floor while the Catchers search. Hiders grab them: each nut is worth 10 bolts, up to 50 a match (100 in Free Roam). Get caught and you drop half. Whatever is in your pocket at the end is yours.

Lucky finds

Stay in a hiding spot for 5 seconds and there's a 1 in 10 chance of finding a special item tucked inside. One lucky find a match.

Claw machine

2 bolts a try, 3 tries a match, and about 1 win in 5. It flashes and jingles while you play, so the Catchers can hear you.

Vending machine

A sticky note with a 3-digit code is hidden in one hiding spot. Hide there to find it. Type the code and you get 8 seconds of safety to pick any Common or Rare item. No code? Pay 100 bolts instead, with no safety. One item a match.

All 28 items

Wardrobe, bolts and daily challenges

Dress your pet from head to tail: a hat, glasses, a collar and something on its back, all worn at once. 26 items to collect, and every one moves with your pet as it runs, hops and turns. Other players see your outfit online.

Earn bolts by playing

  • +5 bolts for every match you finish
  • +10 more when your side wins
  • +5 more for surviving the final 30 seconds
  • Five new daily challenges every day
  • Buy up to five bonus challenge slots for tougher goals
  • +60 bolts each when your Pack finishes its weekly Pack Heist
  • 10 bolts for every bolt token you grab (up to 50 a match)
  • Gift codes from The Data Wizard

Spend bolts in the Wardrobe, on claw machine tries and on vending machine items. In the Wardrobe, hover over any item to try it on before you buy. Goldfish can't wear collars or back items (the bowl's in the way), and snakes can't wear back items.

Play online

Make a room and share its code, or browse open games. Up to 8 players per room, with computer pets filling the gaps. Chat is filtered and players can be reported. Your progress is saved on the i-Pet server.

The host can switch on Special items & machines. Everyone brings their equipped item, and bolt tokens, the claw and vending machines and lucky finds work for every player. These games don't count for Paw Points, so the leaderboards stay fair. Pack-only games always keep special items off.

A name bubble floats over every pet: players show their tag, Pack tag and rank, and computer pets get their own made-up tags starting with "cbot". A hidden pet on the other side never shows its bubble, so tags can't give anyone away. Turn bubbles to "Players only" or off in Settings.

Paw Points and season ranks

Online matches earn Paw Points. Climb six ranks every monthly "Stocktake" season. Your rank shows as a coloured dot next to your name.

How you earn points

Matches must last at least a minute with 2 or more players. Up to 150 points per match and 1,500 a day.

Pet of the Week

Weekly and season leaderboards, with tabs for Hiders, Catchers and Packs. You always see the top 10 and the pets just above and below you.

Last week's number one appears as a golden hologram statue above the Mega Store fountain, wearing their hat, for every player to see.

Packs

  • Build a crew of 6–40 pets with its own patch and invite code
  • Pack chat, Pack-only games and gifts between mates
  • A weekly Pack Heist goal earns bolts for everyone
  • Choose one of four factions and compete for weekly points and prizes
  • Turf Wars: the Pack with the most points on a map that week flies its banner there

You can play without registering. An account keeps your saved game profile on the server so it can sync between devices.

Questions

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Every mode works offline with computer pets as Hiders and Catchers. Pick an easy, normal or hard difficulty, and up to 30 computer teammates.

Who are the "cbot" players?

They're the computer pets. Each one gets a random tag like cbot4821 in its name bubble, so you can tell who's who in a crowded match.

How many people can play online?

Up to 8 players in a room. Computer pets fill the empty places so matches always feel busy.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without one. To sync your saved game profile between devices, register here or in the game. Verify your email when you register; use an address you can access. Sign into the updated game once to link its local profile to an account created here.

How do I reset my password?

Choose Sign in, then Forgot password. A reset code is sent to the email address on your account. Reset email delivery depends on the game server's email settings.

Is online chat safe?

Chat is filtered, quick-chat phrases are built in, and players can be reported or removed from a room by its host.

Which modes can I play online?

Classic matches are online today. Every mode can be played offline.

What are bolts for?

Bolts buy Wardrobe items, claw machine tries and vending machine items. You earn them by finishing matches, winning, surviving the final 30 seconds, daily challenges, your Pack's weekly goal and bolt tokens you grab in a match.

What are gift codes?

12-digit codes The Data Wizard shares for bolts, special items or Wardrobe items. Redeem one on your Profile page while you're online. Each code works once, for one player, and runs out after its end date.

Can I buy special items?

No. Nothing in the game costs real money. You win special items at the claw machine, pick them at the vending machine or find them in hiding spots.

Are special items fair online?

They're off unless the host switches them on, and games with special items don't count for Paw Points. Every item has a counter, so the other side can fight back.
